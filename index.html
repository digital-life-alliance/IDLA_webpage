Charting the Digital Future of Life Sciences

Declaration of The International
Digital Life Alliance(IDLA)

The International Digital Life Alliance (IDLA), a global membership organisation, is the collective voice for the advancement of digital life sciences and technologies. It brings together national and regional bodies, research institutions, and industry leaders to foster a worldwide, interdisciplinary approach to the integration of digital innovation with human, environmental, and societal well-being.

IDLA is the international alliance dedicated to unifying the diverse disciplines of artificial intelligence, biotechnology, digital health, data science, and ethics under one collaborative framework elected and recognised by its peers across sectors. It is a voluntary, non-profit federation of member organisations committed to linking digital life innovators, scientists, engineers, policymakers, and communities worldwide.

TIDLA promotes the advancement of global digital life technologies through education initiatives, collaborative research, leadership opportunities, specialised symposia, policy dialogues, and engagement with other international and intergovernmental organisations.

The Alliance fosters the worldwide exchange of knowledge and ideas through regular World Digital Life Congresses, expert academies, scholarly publications, open-access digital repositories, regular technology bulletins, monthly news briefs, global webinars, and active participation in social and professional networks.

GOALS

Open Biological &
Health Data Infrastructure

Build and maintain interoperable, secure, and ethical global platforms for sharing biological, clinical, and environmental health data — enabling transparent, equitable access for research, education, and public benefit.

AI‑Driven
Scientific Discovery

Harness artificial intelligence, machine learning, and advanced analytics to accelerate breakthroughs in life sciences, medicine, agriculture, environmental sustainability, and bioengineering — ensuring explainability and accountability in all applications.

Digital Health &
Resilient Societies

Promote development and deployment of inclusive, scalable digital health technologies that strengthen health systems, improve early disease detection and prevention, and build resilience against global health challenges.

Ethics, Standards & Governance for Digital Life

Define and uphold internationally recognised ethical principles, technical standards, and governance frameworks for the safe, transparent, and trustworthy use of digital life technologies.

Global Collaboration & Capacity Building

Connect researchers, innovators, educators, policymakers, and communities worldwide through training, policy dialogue, innovation hubs, and knowledge exchange — ensuring that digital life benefits are accessible to all nations and peoples.

Founding Institution

Guangzhou Laboratory, established in 2021 in Guangzhou, China, is a pioneering research institution dedicated to advancing respiratory diseases and their prevention. The lab cultivates a dynamic, multidisciplinary team to unravel the pathogenesis of respiratory infections and chronic conditions, while drive breakthroughs in therapeutic development, vaccine innovation, and prevention technologies. Through integrated research platforms, Guangzhou Lab accelerates the translation of scientific discoveries into transformative solutions.

With a mission rooted in scientific rigor, global impact, and technological foresight, Guangzhou Laboratory serves as a founding institution of the International Digital Life Alliance, working to shape a healthier and more resilient future through digital life sciences.
